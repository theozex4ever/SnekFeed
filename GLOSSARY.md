# Snek Feed

The language of caring for Snek. These entries describe feeding.

## Language

**Feeding**:
The lifecycle of a snack offered to Snek, from arrival through meal credit or cancellation.

**Outstanding snack**:
A snack offered to Snek that has neither received meal credit nor been cancelled, whether falling, waiting, or claimed.
_Avoid_: Floor snack when including falling or claimed snacks

**Claimed snack**:
An outstanding snack that Snek has begun chewing but has not yet received meal credit.
_Avoid_: Eaten snack before meal credit

**Tummy reservation**:
The tummy fill committed to an outstanding snack, including a claimed snack.
_Avoid_: Pending fill when excluding claimed snacks

**Meal credit**:
The recognition of a completed snack in Snek's tummy fill, mood, growth, and eaten-snack count.

**Cancelled snack**:
A snack discarded before meal credit.

**Butterfly feast**:
A golden butterfly reward consisting of three free snacks.

**Auto Bowl**:
A care upgrade that spends hearts to offer a snack when Snek is peckish and no outstanding snack remains.
